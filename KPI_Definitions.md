# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Abbreviation | Working Definition | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net Interest Income | NII | Interest income earned less interest expense | Measures core banking revenue and sensitivity to rates, loan growth, and funding costs |
| Non-Interest Income | — | Revenue from fees, trading, insurance, and other non-interest activities | Shows revenue diversification beyond lending spreads |
| Provision for Credit Losses | PCL | Expense recognized for expected credit losses | Affects earnings and signals expected credit deterioration |
| Allowance for Credit Losses | ACL | Balance-sheet reserve against expected credit losses | Indicates reserves available to absorb expected losses |
| Gross Impaired Loans | GIL | Loans identified as credit-impaired | Measures the stock of problem loans |
| Common Equity Tier 1 Ratio | CET1 | Highest-quality regulatory capital relative to risk-weighted assets | Key measure of loss-absorbing capital strength |
| Risk-Weighted Assets | RWA | Assets weighted according to regulatory risk | Denominator used in regulatory capital ratios |
| Return on Equity | ROE | Net income available to common shareholders relative to common equity | Measures profitability generated from shareholder capital |
| Efficiency Ratio | — | Non-interest expense divided by total revenue | Lower values generally indicate stronger operating efficiency |
| Operating Leverage | — | Revenue growth minus expense growth | Shows whether revenue is growing faster than costs |
| Liquidity Coverage Ratio | LCR | High-quality liquid assets relative to projected short-term cash outflows | Assesses short-term liquidity resilience |
| Net Stable Funding Ratio | NSFR | Available stable funding relative to required stable funding | Assesses structural funding stability over a one-year horizon |
| Net Write-Offs | NWO | Loans written off, net of recoveries | Measures realized credit losses |
| Pre-Provision, Pre-Tax Earnings | PPPT | Earnings before credit-loss provisions and income taxes | Separates operating earnings from credit and tax effects |
| Reported Results | — | IFRS results without management adjustments | Primary basis for the dashboard and ratio analysis |
| Adjusted Results | — | Management-defined results excluding specified items | Supplementary view that should not be mixed with reported results |
