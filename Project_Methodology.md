# Project Methodology

## Scope

This project evaluates Royal Bank of Canada's financial performance, operating efficiency, credit risk, balance-sheet trends, capital adequacy, liquidity, and business-segment performance for FY2023–FY2025 and Q1 2024–Q2 2026.

## Source Collection

Publicly available RBC Investor Relations documents were used, including annual reports, quarterly reports to shareholders, supplementary financial information, Pillar 3 disclosures, and earnings releases.

## Raw Data Capture

Separate worksheets were used for annual performance, quarterly performance, balance-sheet data, credit quality, capital and liquidity, and segment results.

## Standardization

Raw information was reorganized into standardized schedules. Amounts are presented in CAD millions unless otherwise stated. Metrics that were not separately disclosed were left blank instead of being replaced with zero.

## KPI Analysis

The model evaluates revenue, net interest income, non-interest income, net income, diluted EPS, ROE, efficiency ratio, operating leverage, PPPT, PCL, impaired loans, ACL, net write-offs, CET1, total capital, LCR, and NSFR.

## Segment Analysis

The model compares Personal Banking, Commercial Banking, Wealth Management, Insurance, Capital Markets, and Corporate Support.

## Interactive Analysis

The workbook includes dropdown-based KPI selection, segment-period selection, advanced formulas, normalized PivotTable data, a native PivotTable, and slicers.

## Validation

The data-audit worksheet reviews revenue, pre-tax income, net income, PPPT, segment totals, loan balances, capital ratios, liquidity thresholds, and formula-error indicators.

## Limitations

The project relies on publicly disclosed information. Some segment metrics are not separately reported. Blank values should not be interpreted as zero. The project is historical and is not an investment recommendation.
