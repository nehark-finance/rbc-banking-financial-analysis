# Source Register

The project uses official Royal Bank of Canada Investor Relations disclosures.

Primary public source page:

https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/financial-information.html

| Source ID | Document | Reporting Period | Document Type | Main Information Used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AR-25 | RBC 2025 Annual Report | FY2025 | Audited Annual Report | Annual statements, MD&A, notes, and accounting policies |
| SUP-25Q4-PDF | RBC Q4 2025 Financial Supplement | Q4/FY2025 | Financial Supplement | Annual and quarterly financial schedules |
| SUP-25Q4-XLS | RBC Q4 2025 Financial Supplement | Q4/FY2025 | Excel Supplement | Extractable annual and quarterly information |
| QR-26Q2 | RBC Q2 2026 Report to Shareholders | Q2 2026 | Interim Report | Latest results, financial statements, and MD&A |
| SUP-26Q2-PDF | RBC Q2 2026 Financial Supplement | Q2 2026 | Financial Supplement | Quarterly trends, segment, credit, and capital data |
| SUP-26Q2-XLS | RBC Q2 2026 Financial Supplement | Q2 2026 | Excel Supplement | Extractable quarterly information |
| P3-26Q2-PDF | RBC Q2 2026 Pillar 3 Disclosure | Q2 2026 | Regulatory Disclosure | Regulatory capital, RWA, and risk exposure |
| P3-26Q2-XLS | RBC Q2 2026 Pillar 3 Quantitative Tables | Q2 2026 | Regulatory Tables | Capital and regulatory-risk information |
| PR-26Q2 | RBC Q2 2026 Earnings Release | Q2 2026 | Earnings Release | Executive financial highlights |

The original RBC source documents are not included in the repository. The repository contains the independent workbook, screenshots, documentation, and a link to RBC's public source page.
